Methodology

Forecast protocol

Every model is initialized from the same ERA5 (ARCO) analysis at 00z and integrated 20 steps × 6 h to 5 days inside NVIDIA Earth2Studio. No model sees its own analysis — identical inputs, identical horizon.

State-variable scores

Latitude-weighted RMSE, bias, and anomaly correlation ACC (against the WeatherBench2 climatology) for T2M, T850, Z500, MSL, U10 and V10, on the native 0.25° grid.

Precipitation scores

6-h accumulations scored with CSI and FSS at 1, 5 and 10 mm thresholds (FSS over a 9-cell ≈ 250 km neighborhood), plus RMSE and bias in mm.

Regions

Global, NH extratropics (20°–90° N), tropics (20° S–20° N), and SH extratropics (90°–20° S) — all area-weighted.

Baseline

Persistence — the initial state carried forward unchanged — is the no-skill reference every model must beat. It runs through the identical scoring pipeline.

Tiers

Init times older than 6 days are the historic regime: ERA5 init and ERA5 truth, tier FINAL. A real-time regime (GFS init, IMERG truth) is planned as Phase 2.
